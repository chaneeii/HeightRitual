DM2601 · Group 6

Memory Meter

People move. Memories stay.

An interactive hi-fi prototype for the ritual of measuring height: an iOS app, a Bluetooth controller box and a projector that shows every past record life-size on any wall.

Anna Nolden, Chanhee Jeong, Montana Eriksson, Szu-Yi Ho · DM2601 Media Technology and Interaction Design, KTH

01

Install the app on an iPhone

Requires an iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No account needed besides your Apple ID.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite link below on the iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. The app appears as Memory Meter.

Without the projector and controller, the app runs on the phone alone and shows a copy of the wall. To try it quickly: Settings › Load sample family, then open Memory.
