DM2601 · Group 6

Memory Meter

People move. Memories stay.

An iOS app, a Bluetooth controller and a projector that show every past height record life-size on any wall.

Anna Nolden, Chanhee Jeong, Montana Eriksson, Szu-Yi Ho
DM2601 Media Technology and Interaction Design, KTH

01 Install the app

Try it on an iPhone

iPhone with iOS 17 or later. No account needed besides your Apple ID.

  1. Install TestFlight from the App Store.
  2. Open the invite link on the iPhone.
  3. Tap Accept, then Install. The app appears as Memory Meter.

No projector or controller? The app shows a copy of the wall on the phone. Try Settings › Load sample family, then open Memory.
